This [violin plot](https://scanpy.readthedocs.io/en/stable/generated/scanpy.pl.violin.html) displays the expression of one gene per cell type (`bulk_labels`) in single-cell transcriptomics data obtained from Scanpy's [example datasets](https://scanpy.readthedocs.io/en/stable/generated/scanpy.datasets.pbmc68k_reduced.html).
Each violin's width shows how many of its cells have a given expression value, after excluding outliers beyond three standard deviations from the mean.
The line within each violin spans the interquartile range, and is crossed at the median.
Scroll or drag to zoom and pan along the expression axis.
Use the controls to choose another gene, or to exclude cells with zero expression.
